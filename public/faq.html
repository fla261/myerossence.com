Frequently Asked Questions

About MyErossence

What is MyErossence?

MyErossence is a hair care product review and recommendation site. We research and curate premium hair care products to help you find what works best for your hair type and needs.

Do you sell products directly?

No. We are a review and recommendation site. When you find a product you like, we link you to trusted retailers (like Amazon) where you can purchase it directly.

How do you choose which products to feature?

We evaluate products based on ingredient quality, user reviews, brand reputation, and real-world results. We focus on premium, clean formulations from established brands.

Shopping & Links

Are the prices on your site accurate?

We do our best to keep prices current, but prices are set by the retailers and may change. Always check the final price on the retailer's website before purchasing.

What are affiliate links?

Affiliate links are special tracking links. When you click a product link on our site and make a purchase, we may earn a small commission at no extra cost to you. This helps support our site. See our Affiliate Disclosure for full details.

Does clicking an affiliate link cost me more?

No. The price you pay is exactly the same whether you use our link or go directly to the retailer's site.

Hair Care

How do I know which products are right for my hair type?

Each product on our site includes information about which hair types it works best for. You can also filter products by hair type on our Shop page, or read our blog articles for detailed guides.

Are the products you recommend natural/organic?

We prioritize products with clean, high-quality ingredients. Many of the brands we feature are known for natural, vegan, and cruelty-free formulations. However, "natural" claims vary — always check the ingredient list on the product page.

Contact & Support

How can I contact you?

Email us at hello@myerossence.com. We typically respond within 24-48 hours.

Can I suggest a product for review?

Yes! We welcome product suggestions. Send us an email and we'll consider it for a future feature or review.

I have an issue with a product I purchased — can you help?

Since we don't sell products directly, you'll need to contact the retailer where you made the purchase (e.g., Amazon) for order-related issues like returns, refunds, or shipping.
